Legend

            

              
Active / hole cells

              
Active cells are partitioned by the solver; holes are ignored.

              
Cycle symbols

              
Cell symbols feed Rose Windows.

              
Walls

              
Press on a corner and drag along grid lines; walls appear as you pass each corner. A stroke that starts on an existing wall erases.

              
Join border

              
Marks a border as known to be inside one region (no wall).

              
Gemini / Delta / Difference / Inequality

              
Click an edge or two edge-adjacent cells to relate their eventual regions.

              
Area number clue

              
Places a fixed region-size clue on one cell.

              
Polyomino clue

              
Places a drawn shape clue on one cell.

              
Palisade / Compass clues

              
Place cell clues for side-border patterns or directional counts.

              
Watchtower clue

              
Click a grid vertex to count distinct regions touching that corner.

              
Hotkeys (hover the board)

              
Right-click toggles a cell; drag corner to corner for given borders. W then 1-5 Rose Window; D / G Delta / Gemini on a border; P Polyomino; # then 0-4 Palisade; X then 0-9 Compass (side nearest the pointer); 0-9 over a cell Area Number; 0-9 over a border Difference; 1-4 over a corner Watchtower; arrow keys over a border Inequality (arrow points at the smaller region); Delete removes the clue; Esc cancels a W / # / X mode.

              
Boundary graph

              
Shows selected solution borders and highlights degree 3 or degree 4 vertices for Loopy and Bricky debugging.

              
Screenshot tracing

              
Use an image as a manual tracing layer behind the grid.
